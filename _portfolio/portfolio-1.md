## Research Methodology

My research follows a **data-driven and interdisciplinary methodology**, combining representation learning, clustering, statistical validation, and domain expertise.

The general workflow I use for developing new deep clustering methods starts with the **collection, governance, processing, and curation of multimodal data**, followed by **representation learning** to extract compact and informative features/embeddings. Clustering methods are then applied to discover meaningful structures, which are evaluated through quantitative metrics and statistical analysis. Finally, the resulting clusters are interpreted with **domain experts**, with Large Language Models potentially supporting interactive analysis and knowledge validation.

![Deep Clustering Methodology](/images/my_methodology.jpeg)

This methodology is designed to move beyond purely numerical clustering evaluation by connecting **learned representations and discovered structures with domain knowledge and human interpretation**. The ultimate goal is to develop clustering methods that produce not only statistically meaningful groups, but also **interpretable and actionable insights**.

## Research Philosophy

I believe that impactful AI research requires more than powerful models. It requires **solid data, meaningful representations, rigorous evaluation, and human-centered interpretation**.

My goal is to develop AI methods that can transform complex and high-dimensional data into **reliable, interpretable, and useful knowledge**.
