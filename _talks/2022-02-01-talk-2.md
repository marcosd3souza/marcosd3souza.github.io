This was my guest lecture for the *Roda de Conversa de Dados* at CESAR. I introduced the audience to the concepts and challenges of feature engineering and presented KNMFS, a novel unsupervised feature selection method derived from my research.

### Topics covered:

- The definition of features and their inherent challenges, including data dispersion and the curse of dimensionality.
- Continuous Integration in Data Science workflows, focusing on Continuous Delivery for Machine Learning (CD4ML) and Feature Stores.
- An in-depth overview of the **KNMFS (Kernelized NMF and Modularity Feature Selection)** method, which is based on iterative similarity graph factorization and clustering by modularity.
- The five-step KNMFS pipeline:
  - **1. Similarity Matrix construction**.
  - **2. Iterative Non-negative Matrix Factorization (NMF)** with a Gaussian filter.
  - **3. K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) Similarity Graph** generation.
  - **4. Louvain Community Detection** for modularity optimization.
  - **5. Feature Scoring** using L2-norm.
- Experimental results on real and synthetic datasets demonstrating the performance of KNMFS against state-of-the-art methods regarding Accuracy, NMI, and ARI metrics.
